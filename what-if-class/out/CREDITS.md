# Credits — "What if you paid attention in class?"

- **Everything visual** is rendered by code: Remotion 4 + Three.js / React Three Fiber. Original blocky characters with 16×16 pixel faces drawn at render time. No AI video, no Minecraft assets, no real app UI.
- **Phone app** "loopr" and the account `@couch.dog.daily` are invented for this short.
- **Voiceover:** ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, voice "Matthew Schmitz – Relaxed Conversational" (ElevenLabs voice library). Word timestamps: ElevenLabs Scribe v1.
- **Music:** the user-supplied track (`music.m4a`, 27.5 s, looped ×3 to 60 s). Beat map supplied by the user.
- **SFX:** synthesized locally with ffmpeg (ding, tap, whoosh, scratch, boom, choir pad, bell).
- **Font:** Montserrat (SIL Open Font License), bundled locally.
