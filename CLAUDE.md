
## Video rules (Le Livre d'Hénoch and other shorts)

- **Hashtags are always part of the caption.** Every episode's `caption.txt` ends its legend (the text pasted on TikTok / Reels / Shorts) with the hashtags on the same post, not only in a separate list.
- **On-screen captions sit in the vertical middle of the frame.** Centre the word captions around y ≈ 960 of 1920 (not the lower third). Keep the speaker tag just above them and the title/question block clear of them.
