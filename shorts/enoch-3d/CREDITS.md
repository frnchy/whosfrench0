# Credits — Le Livre d'Hénoch, épisodes 1–2 (3D)

- **Visuals**: 100% code-built 3D (Three.js via @remotion/three in Remotion 4): procedural characters (rigged figures, faces with blinking eyes and lip-sync, wings, robes), sets (heaven above the clouds, Mount Hermon, the village, the forge) and effects. No AI images, no AI video, no Higgsfield.
- **Voices**: ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, library voices — Justin Doré (narrator Hénoch), Olivier D (Shemihaza), Julien (Azazel), Leon (the nervous angel), David (the smith; chorus), Natacha (the woman), Dave (Kokabiel).
- **Word timing / lip-sync**: ElevenLabs Scribe (`eleven_scribe_v1`) per line; mouth openness from each line's loudness envelope.
- **Music**: ElevenLabs Music v2.5 (original cues per episode). **SFX**: ElevenLabs Sound Effects v2; wind bed and low rumble synthesized with ffmpeg.
- **Fonts**: Inter, EB Garamond (SIL Open Font License, via @fontsource).
- **Text**: R. H. Charles, *The Book of Enoch* (1912/1917), public domain — see `plan/ep*/research.md`.
