# Research — Le Livre d'Hénoch, résumés des épisodes 1–8

Primary source: R. H. Charles, *The Book of Enoch* (Oxford, 1912; SPCK edition 1917), the standard English translation of the
Ethiopic text (1 Enoch). Chapter and verse numbers below follow Charles. The series and these recaps are a dramatisation:
each line is marked **textuel** (stated in the text) or **dramatisation** (added for the dialogue, not in the text).
